How we work

We use existing AI models for specific functions, and each product works under its own rules.

GrossKeep operates through deterministic rules pre-approved by the dealer. Scheduled alerts and reminders are sent automatically based on configured parameters, without requiring individual manual validation for each message.

SharKeep is designed to automatically respond to inquiries received through your dealership's social media channels, following configured rules, instructions, and limits. It currently operates as a prototype in testing with one dealership, distinguishing intended capabilities from features already implemented and validated. Cases requiring special attention may be referred to a person, depending on the enabled rules and features.

We design tools to highlight cases that need attention and identify each product by its actual development status.
